Glossy, precious, mysterious, dark overall — mostly deep navy with gem highlights, not bright.

Style: crisp pixel art, stepped hard edges, limited palette of blues, same chunky logical pixel density as a 16-bit RPG (each logical pixel about 6x6 output pixels), no smooth gradients, no photorealism.
