red-field-front
Use case: stylized-concept. Create exactly ONE complete standalone production enemy sprite for a Deltarune-like low-resolution pixel RPG. The attached image is ONLY a pixel density/style reference, NOT character identity or layout. Subject is the familiar LEAGUE OF LEGENDS MELEE LANE MINION (CS), a small squat stocky helmeted armored foot soldier, NOT a caster, siege cannon or yellow cartoon Minion. Oversized team-colored enclosed metal helmet/hood, dark recessed visor with two tiny bright eyes, chunky shoulder armor, squat torso, short armored boots, a short-handled chunky iron warhammer and a small shield. Simple silver/steel trim. Friendly-to-read silly little grunt proportions, not a tall human knight. No human face or hair. Crisp hard square pixel clusters, strongly stepped dark outline, extremely limited flat colors, 2 shade steps per material. Roughly 32-40 logical pixels tall, enlarged as nearest-neighbor blocks; absolutely no micro-detail, smooth outline, gradients, antialiasing, soft shading, painterly texture, bloom or 3D rendering. Entire helmet, weapon, shield and feet inside central65% of square image, full-body, ample empty margin on every side. Background 100% solid flat #FF00FF magenta, no floor, ground shadow, scenery, text, labels, panels, grid or UI.
Red team: deep crimson and bright red cloth/helmet/armor with neutral steel trim. FIELD SPRITE: strict straight FRONT view, facing toward the viewer/down-screen, both eyes symmetrically visible, both shoulders visible, relaxed standing posture; warhammer down on viewer's left and shield on viewer's right. Exactly one static full-body front sprite.
