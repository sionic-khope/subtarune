EXACTLY 1 column x 2 rows, 1024x512 canvas, invisible equal 1024x256 cells, no text, no labels, no grid, no borders.
Row 1: the crowd standing calmly, arms down, slight variety in head height.
Row 2: the SAME crowd in the same positions cheering: many arms raised, some heads tilted, glow sticks up.
