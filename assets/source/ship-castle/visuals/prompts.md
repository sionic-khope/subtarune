Tall right-side-wall viewing window, subtly angled perspective so its front glass plane faces left toward the room. Frame in midnight navy gunmetal with silver seams and a few muted brass accents matching the attached purple door and steel warship. A single LARGE CLEAR unobstructed glass pane suitable for two human sprites flying through and breaking it, not dense bars: one restrained horizontal reinforced crossbar at most, beveled steel sill, sturdy complete rim. Soft pale cyan glass reflection inside the pane only, almost transparent, suggesting ocean daylight. Outer space truly transparent. No scenery/room/floor/light beam outside (game will draw sea and subtle sunlight separately), no characters, no UI, no text. Clear chunky pixel clusters, dark crisp outlines, game-native 32-bit pixel art, no soft painting/3D rendering, no blurred edge. Entire frame visible with margin.
